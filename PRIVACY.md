# Vice Space — Privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Vice Space is a private app for keeping messages, photos, documents, notes,
contacts, important dates and birth charts on your own phone.

## What the app stores
Everything you add — messages, photos, documents, notes, contacts, dates, birth details
and charts — is saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

## What the app does not do
- It has no accounts and no servers. Nothing you add is uploaded or shared.
- No advertising, analytics or tracking.

## Internet use
The app may use the internet only to show map tiles and look up place names when you
search for a birthplace, and to load the logo font if it isn't bundled. These requests
contain your search text and map position, not your personal content.

## Astrology
Birth charts are calculated from the details you enter. Interpretations are traditional
and are not facts, advice or predictions.

## Deleting your data
Delete individual items in the app, use Settings → *Delete my private notes and files*,
or uninstall the app to remove everything from the device. Android may include app data
in your device backup only if you allow it; Vice Space opts out of cloud backup.

## Contact
Use the developer email address on the Google Play listing.
